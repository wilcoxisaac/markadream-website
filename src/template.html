Home / About

Our story

Built for the builders capital overlooks

Mark A Dream began with a simple observation: the businesses that hold communities together, the family-owned company, the well-kept rental, the founder with a real product, are often the ones institutional capital overlooks. They are too small, too local, or too patient for a fund on a seven-year clock.

We built Mark A Dream to be the partner those builders were missing. We invest our own and our partners' capital, which means we can say yes to the right deal at the right pace and hold it for as long as it keeps creating value.

Mission

To create opportunity and lasting wealth by backing the people who build, own, and care for real businesses and real places.

Vision

A portfolio of enduring companies and properties that provides for families, employs neighbors, and funds the next generation of dreamers.

Our values spell our name

  • Discipline. Position size is set before price is negotiated.
  • Responsibility. Commitments to employees and tenants are written into our terms.
  • Endurance. Every decision is tested against a 25-year horizon.
  • Alignment. Our capital goes in first, alongside yours.
  • Multigenerational. Wealth and values that outlast a single cycle.

Principles

Four rules that govern every decision

The guardrails behind every allocation choice above.

Disciplined allocation

No single investment exceeds a set share of the balance sheet. Position size is decided before price is negotiated.

Diversification with intent

We diversify across lanes and geographies, but not into things we don't understand. Treasury assets keep us liquid and patient.

Responsible growth

Modest leverage, positive cash flow, and growth funded by profits. A business that survives a downturn compounds longer.

Long-term vision

We underwrite every deal as if we will own it for 25 years. If it only works with a quick flip, we pass.

How co-investing works

We build relationships first. Investors join a private list, meet the team, and receive details of specific opportunities only after we have confirmed accreditation and a pre-existing relationship. Every offering is made through formal documents prepared by counsel, never through this website.

Who qualifies as accredited?

Under SEC rules, individuals generally qualify with income above $200,000 ($300,000 with a spouse or partner) in each of the last two years, net worth above $1 million excluding a primary residence, or by holding a Series 7, 65, or 82 license. Read the full guide →

Questions investors ask

Typical minimums?

Set per deal, commonly $25,000–$50,000.

How long is capital committed?

Real estate deals typically hold 5–10 years; private business stakes may be longer. Liquidity is limited.

How are fees structured?

Disclosed in each offering's documents. We favor structures where we earn most of our return only after investors receive theirs.
